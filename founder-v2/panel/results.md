These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## Why they buy

| reason | buyers | in their words |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| convenience | 7 | "I have to buy a site anyway, it is on my consultant's list, and this is the first offer where I don't have to write a single text or chase anyone for an acceptance report; 800 out of a 46,000 grant is not where I will lose sleep. I'd forward it to my consultant tonight and if she says the invoice wording works, I sign." (P002) · "I have to buy a site anyway and document it, and this is the first offer that says the words my file uses: acceptance report, dated screenshots, invoice matching the contract, funding page in the programme format. He writes the texts from a phone call, which is the part I would never find an evening for." (P003) |
| trust | 1 | "I have to spend this line anyway and what scares me is not the site, it is the file. He has filled in the same AFIR reports, he gives me the proces-verbal, dated screenshots and an invoice worded like my contract, and the funding page in the required format, so I am paying 800 for the paperwork being right more than for the pages. My cousin would do it for 300 but I am not sure his invoice and a handwritten 'reception' would pass the check, and I cannot afford a rejected expense." (P001) |
| timing | 1 | "My first progress report is due in six weeks and the website line has to be spent and proven by then; 10 days, the funding page in the programme format, dated screenshots and an invoice worded like my contract is exactly the pile of paper my consultant keeps asking me for. My cousin would do it for 300 but he cannot give me an acceptance report or an invoice from a firm that passes the file check." (P007) |

## What would flip a no

- If my consultant said he is fine and that his acceptance report and invoice wording have already passed a Start-Up Nation reimbursement check without questions, I would sign the same day.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
