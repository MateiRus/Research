# Brief for the board (second evaluation, plan v4 hardened)

Read this on its own; you cannot see the conversation it came from.

## Context: what the first board and the first buyer panel said

A first version of this business (a professional website plus two automations sold to grant consultants at 2,400 EUR + 140 EUR/month, with a funding-call "radar" as upsell) was evaluated by a board (three lenses, 3 × FUND IF, average 4.3/10: the product was a commodity, no proof, three products for one part-time person, the AI eligibility pre-check unmeasured) and by a simulated buyer panel: 0 of 20 bought; a re-test with only the document-collection automation at 900 EUR + 50 EUR/month, month-to-month, with continuity clauses, got 2 of 20 (both firms with 50+ clients); 14 of 20 refused on trust ("a part-time stranger with my clients' files and no reference I can call"), the website was wanted by nobody, small consultancies (5–15 clients a year) collect documents by hand and will not pay.

The founder then ran two lateral-thinking sessions (concept fan, assumption inversion) and three rounds of adversarial "investor" review. The result is the plan below.

## The founder

A 2nd-year Economic Informatics student in Bucharest with a day job as a junior Oracle APEX / PL/SQL developer, from Oradea (Bihor), 10–12 h/week for the business, 1–2 trips a month to Bihor (plus holidays at home). Skills: SQL/PLSQL/APEX, n8n, Python, WordPress, HTML/CSS/JS, LLM APIs. The firm (ILLUSTRUS S.R.L., Bihor) was financed by a LEADER/AFIR start-up grant and must earn at least 10,000 EUR of recognised web-service revenue within 3 years; target month 13 (range 12–15). The founder is himself a grant beneficiary and knows the paperwork, the GAL and the consultant who wrote his plan.

**Rules:** no source above 40% of revenue; 42 h/month delivery cap plus 4–5 h/month reserve (15%); gates at day 30 (≥2 agencies accepting a task, ≥5 beneficiaries identified and 2 talked to, pilot signed; otherwise targets shift between A and B), month 6 (≥10 SUN beneficiaries contracted in Bihor or B stays on DR36/PR NV), month 12 (if recurring + licensed kits ≥ 400 EUR/month continue at 5 h/week; otherwise maintenance-only and clean closure after the monitoring period). Continuity: everything on client accounts; named backup person may take over maintenance contracts.

**Money:** 10,000 EUR in month 13 (12–15); 440 h + 66 h reserve; external costs ~1,800 EUR (hosting, VPS, travel ~80 EUR/month, bookkeeping); margin before the founder's time ~8,200 EUR ≈ 18 EUR/h; after month 15: 3 published case studies, the licensable kit, ~250–400 EUR/month recurring.

**Market facts (public, October 2026):** Bihor has ~400–470 consulting firms (60–70% in Oradea); junior developers in Romania charge 20–28 EUR/h, freelancers 30–40; agencies prefer structured white-label partners to freelancers with variable availability; Start-Up Nation 2025–2026 grants up to 50,000 EUR with "presentation websites" among eligible expenses; MIPE's 2026 calendar lists ~300 calls but postponements are common; WhatsApp Business utility messages cost ~0.033 USD each in Romania; n8n workflow templates sell at 19–79 USD on marketplaces.

**Still unknown:** number of contracted DR36 beneficiaries within reach; whether any agency will accept; the employment contract's clauses; no client signed yet.
