# Monopoly lens
Score: 4 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The market is finally small and named: grant-funded projects in Bihor, bought through a GAL, a consultant and a handful of Oradea agencies. The Monopoly lens wants a small market first; v1 had none, v4 has one.
- There is a real, if thin, secret: the founder is a beneficiary of the same grant scheme, knows the GAL, the consultant and the paperwork. The "technical delivery documents" (acceptance report with URL, dated screenshots, invoice text matching the contract) are know-how that a 389 EUR template shop does not sell. That, not the website, is the differentiating unit.
- Distribution is at least designed rather than assumed: white-label through agencies, a referrer on commission, a GAL list, Start-Up Nation beneficiaries as a timed cohort. Someone else pays for the website (eligible expense), which answers the panel's "nobody wanted the website".
- Trust objection from the buyer panel is addressed structurally: everything on client accounts, named backup, refund guarantee, no files held by the firm.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No moat of any kind.** Of the four durable advantages the lens names (10x technology, network effects, scale, brand) the plan has none and does not say which it would build. Each line sits in a crowded market with published prices: websites at 389–990 EUR, automation agencies at 300–1,500 EUR, n8n templates at 19–79 USD. A copycat in month six is any Oradea agency adding "funding-visibility page + acceptance report" to its template, and the plan itself proposes licensing the kit to agencies at 300 EUR, handing the only know-how to the competitors with distribution.
2. **Distribution is still a hypothesis.** The first 100 customers question has no answer; the first 5 do not either. Zero clients signed, agency acceptance unknown, DR36 count unknown, Start-Up Nation buyers arrive only after contracting and postponements are common. The 10,000 EUR target in month 13 depends on three unproven channels at once.
3. **Three products still, for 10–12 hours a week.** The first board flagged three products for one part-time person; v4 renamed them but kept three. Dividing 440 hours across agencies, beneficiaries and consultants means dominating none of the three small markets, which is the opposite of what this lens asks.

Does v4 answer the earlier findings? Trust: yes, structurally. Website wanted by nobody: yes, by changing who pays. Small consultancies will not pay: yes, 20+ clients only. Commodity: no, it is now three commodities with better paperwork. Three products: no.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Which of the three lines would you keep if forced to keep one, and why is that the market you can own in Bihor before anyone notices?
- How many DR36/LEADER beneficiaries are contracted within reach, today? This number decides line B and is asked for in week 1; why not before the board?
- Who else sells "grant-compliant project website with delivery documents" to the same beneficiary, and what does the consultant currently recommend?
- Why license the kit to agencies at 300 EUR rather than keep it the thing only ILLUSTRUS installs?
- What does the employment contract say about APEX, IP and side work? This is a week-1 unknown that could remove the founder's strongest skill from the plan.
- What happens to the 10% referrer when the consultant's own interest in the pilot conflicts with the reference?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Day-30 gate passed with evidence: one agency has accepted a paid task (not the free one), the DR36 count is known, and the pilot is signed.
2. One wedge declared as the thing to own: the grant-compliant project website with delivery documents, positioned to beneficiaries and their consultants in Bihor; A and C become feeders, not equals.
3. No licensing of the kit to agencies until at least four paid installs and one measured pilot result exist.
4. Employment contract clauses confirmed in writing before any APEX work is offered.
5. A written answer to "what stops a copycat in month six", even if the honest answer is "nothing but the references".
