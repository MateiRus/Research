**8 buy · 2 pass** (80% buy) out of 10 simulated buyers. Seed 11, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- A client with a grant-funded project asking me, in writing, for the visibility page or a reporting dashboard that they are obliged to have and will pay for; then the free first task would make it a no-risk try.
- A booking system on the menu and proof he has delivered for two or three agencies my size without missing a deadline; I'd take the free 10-hour task to check, but I'd only pay after that.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
