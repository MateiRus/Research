# The Monopoly lens: A
Score: 5 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- The market is small and countable: independent MM cabinets in Oradea and the North-West. The founder can go to them in person. Early distribution is cheap.
- There is a real angle: **arm independents against MedLife.** MedLife already gives employers a real-time portal, and it bought the biggest MM provider in Bihor. An independent cabinet without such a portal loses on perception. That is the possible secret.
- The pain sits with the employer (fines of 4,000–8,000 lei), and the cabinet sells the portal on as its own value.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Copycat in month six:** at least six MM programs already issue the fitness certificate, and BizMedica already imports from Excel. A portal is an incremental feature for them, not a breakthrough. Nothing here is 10x better.
2. **Ceiling too low:** at 59–99 EUR, 1,000 EUR MRR needs roughly 10–17 cabinets. Only ~6 are identified in Oradea, and their ownership and software are unverified.
3. No evidence that an independent cabinet pays for this (Gaps).

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many independent cabinets in the North-West are still independent, and what software do they use? (competitor scan)
- Would the MM vendor rather partner with you, or copy you?
- What do you build that stays yours if BizMedica adds a portal?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- 3 paid pre-sales from cabinets before writing code.
- A checked list of ≥20 independent cabinets reachable in the North-West.

# The Monopoly lens: G
Score: 6 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **No documented competitor:** the brief found no "lab → doctor → recall" process in Romania. The large networks build in-house only for themselves, so the independents are an orphan market. Here the lens finds the closest thing to a real secret.
- The evidence that the gap exists elsewhere is solid (6.8–62% of abnormal results not followed up). A person acting beats a passive alert by 73.4% vs. 52.2%, which matches the design of the call list.
- **Proprietary know-how can be built:** parsers for exports from Romanian programs with no API. This is tedious work, and the tedium is a barrier to entry. Closed-loop data is the credible bridge toward preventive health.
- The free audit is a good distribution mechanism: it shows money left on the table.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. The gap does not exist in Romania in a size clinics will pay for (no local measurements).
2. **MDR / Legea 190:** the line between "flagged by the lab" and clinical interpretation, and consent for messages, need legal confirmation.
3. Too few buyers locally: 3 clinics plus 1 lab in Oradea. At 129 EUR you need roughly 8–23 locations, so expansion outside Bihor is needed early.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What do the 6-month audits show at GrandMed/NewMedics/Medena? (real pilot)
- What happens when the clinic's software vendor adds a "scheduled check-ups" report?
- Does e-SănătateaMea (Q4 2026) reduce the gap or close it?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
- 2 audits with a measured gap, and 1 conversion to paid.
- A written regulatory opinion that the product stays outside Regula 11.

# The Monopoly lens: B

# The Monopoly lens: H

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)

## Recomandarea mea (the Monopoly lens)

**First product: G.** It is the only idea with something close to a secret: a gap nobody is solving in Romania, served to an orphan market of independents. Its know-how (integrating exports from programs with no API, plus closed-loop data) accumulates into a barrier. It is also the only one that extends naturally toward preventive health. Its weakness is that the gap is unproven locally, so the 6-month audit is the decisive test.

**Second: A.** It has fast, cheap distribution and a clear story against MedLife, but no defensible advantage over existing MM vendors. It makes sense as a source of revenue or as a second module. B belongs as the employer side of A, not as a separate product.

**I would not fund: B, K, H.** In all three the competition is either Excel, existing vendors, or the state, and nothing would stop a copycat in month six.
