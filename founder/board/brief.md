# Brief for the board

Read this on its own; you cannot see the conversation it came from.

## The idea

ILLUSTRUS S.R.L. is a one-person web-services business in Romania. The founder is a 2nd-year Economic Informatics student in Bucharest with a day job as a junior Oracle APEX / PL/SQL developer, originally from Oradea (Bihor county), with 10–12 hours a week for the business and 1–2 trips a month to Bihor. Skills: SQL, PL/SQL, Oracle APEX, n8n automations, Python, HTML/CSS/JS, WordPress, LLM APIs (extraction, agents). Romanian native, English C1, German B1.

The firm must earn at least 10,000 EUR of recognised revenue from web-related services within 3 years (an obligation from a LEADER/AFIR start-up grant); the founder wants it in 12–15 months.

## What it sells, to whom

Target customer: consulting firms in European funds and business consulting (CAEN 7022/7020), 1–15 people, in Bihor (Oradea) and Bucharest. Decision-maker: the owner. Bihor has roughly 400–470 such firms (60–70% in Oradea); Bucharest has thousands. These firms earn fixed fees plus success fees on grant projects worth tens to hundreds of thousands of euros; their core pain points, per the founder's hypotheses (not yet validated with interviews): they learn about new calls and guide amendments late; they spend hours triaging ineligible requests; client documents arrive late and block applications.

Products (prices excl. VAT):
- START: professional website, 6–8 pages, texts written by the founder from two interviews, FAQ written the way people search, structured data, Google Business profile, Search Console, lead form that produces a structured request and a notification. 1,200 EUR + 60 EUR/month (hosting, updates, daily backup, monitoring, 2 hours of support).
- PRO: START plus exactly two automations: (1) lead form → structured request → AI pre-check of eligibility with a stated reason → notification → automatic booking; (2) document collection from the consultant's clients via secure link, with a missing-items list and reminders. 2,400 EUR + 140 EUR/month (4 hours of support, running costs of the automations).
- RADAR pilot (not in the standard offer): for one consultant, 30 days, 1,100 EUR: monitors 3 official funding sources (the MIPE calls API, the Nord-Vest regional programme site, AFIR sessions), matches new calls and amendments against 5 end-client profiles, sends alerts checked by the founder by the next working day. Eligibility judgement stays with the consultant. After the pilot, a monthly fee (reference 280 EUR) is negotiated.
- Launch terms: the first two clients get 20% off the setup (960 / 1,920 EUR) in exchange for a named case study and two introductions. START carries a written specification; the second 50% instalment is not due until the site matches it. 90 days of free fixes. 50% advance, 50% on delivery. 12-month subscription, 30 days' notice. Site and content belong to the client on full payment; the radar engine stays with the firm under licence.

What it does not promise: Google rankings or appearance in AI answers; that the radar catches every call; business results.

## Market facts the founder has gathered (public prices, seen October 2026)

- Oradea agencies publish presentation-site prices of 100–990 EUR (e.g. 389 EUR + 30 EUR/month; from 2,000 lei with 12 months' free hosting; 90 lei/month subscriptions). No Oradea provider publishes a presentation site above ~990 EUR.
- National agencies with published prices: 299–799 EUR (basic), 990–1,690 EUR ("business"), 2,490 EUR ("premium"), from 2,500 EUR (award-winning agency). Market guides 2026: presentation site 500–2,500 EUR, median ~1,000 EUR; maintenance 29–149 EUR/month typical.
- Automation agencies in Romania: 300–700 EUR + 50–150 EUR/month (chatbot, bookings), 500–1,500 EUR per n8n project, 1,500–5,000 EUR for AI projects.
- AI-search optimisation agencies: 650–2,400 EUR/month; Google states no special files or markup are needed for its AI features.
- Funding-call information is available free from national portals, aggregators with newsletters (16,000+ subscribers at one), and monthly PDF bulletins from the regional agency. A paid legal-monitoring product exists at ~150 lei/month. An accounting-CRM vendor (IMFS One) sells a CRM for accounting firms.
- A public AFIR list of consulting firms exists, filterable by county.

## How customers are reached

Phone calls to firms (allowed), in-person meetings in Oradea, events (CFO Conference Oradea 21 Oct 2026, GoTech World Bucharest 10–11 Nov 2026), referrals from the founder's own grant consultant and the local action group (GAL) network, the AFIR consultants list. Cold e-mail and automated outreach are prohibited by Romanian law 506/2004 and will not be used.

## Costs and capacity

Infrastructure ~40 EUR/month (Oracle Always Free, self-hosted n8n on a ~5–10 EUR VPS, LLM API 5–30 EUR, hosting ~5 EUR/client). Travel ~80 EUR/month. Extra bookkeeping ~30 EUR/month. Delivery effort: START 22 h, PRO 45 h, RADAR pilot 30 h. With 10 h/month of selling and 2 h of admin, and contracted support hours used fully (2 h START, 4 h PRO), at five clients about 20 h/month remain for new deliveries, i.e. one PRO every 2–3 months. Target path: five PRO contracts (two at the launch price) reach the 10,000 EUR objective in month 13–16.

## What is still unknown

No customer interviews yet. No signed client. The exact rules of the grant on which revenue counts are being clarified in writing. No portfolio beyond the founder's own site (to be built) and a demo.
