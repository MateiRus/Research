## Why they pass

| reason | buyers | in their words |

| trust | 10 | "The document-upload and reminder automation would genuinely save my team hours, and 2,400 plus 140 a month is nothing against what we bill. But I would be putting my clients' dossiers and my intake flow on a 12-month contract with one man who does this after his day job; if he gets a better offer or burns out, I am the one explaining to 50 clients why the upload link is dead." (P001) · "The 'launch price for the first two clients' tells me nobody in Romania is using this yet, and the person building it does it on the side, 10-12 hours a week, next to a day job. I already lost money once to an agency that vanished; I am not paying 1,200 EUR up front to a one-man evening project with no portfolio." (P002) |
| price | 7 | "Every web guy in Oradea quotes me 400 to 900 EUR for a site, and this is 2,400 plus 140 a month for twelve months, so over 4,000 EUR in the first year for something that openly does not promise me a single new client. (P003) · "My firm brings in about 43,000 EUR a year and this is 4,080 EUR in the first year before VAT, nearly a tenth of my revenue, for a site that replaces one that still works. (P014) · "Every one of my 5-15 clients a year came from someone else's recommendation, and I have never seen a farmer or an SME owner in Bihor pick a grant writer off Google. ## What would flip a no

- A second named person behind the business, or the automations built on an account I own (my own Microsoft 365 / n8n / whatever) with a written handover so that if ILLUSTRUS disappears tomorrow everything keeps running without him.
- A named consulting firm in Bihor or Bucharest that has run this for at least six months, whose owner I can call myself, plus a written continuity plan (who takes over hosting and the automations if he stops).
- Sell me the document-collection and eligibility pre-check automations on their own, around 800 to 1,000 EUR setup and under 60 a month, month-to-month, and show me one grant consultant whose farmer clients actually use the upload link.
- A reference call with a grant-consulting firm like mine that has run both automations for at least six months, plus an option to buy the two automations without the website or with a shorter commitment.
- A named consultant in Bihor or Bucharest with a finished site and six months of the automations running, who I can call and who says the pre-check actually cut their calls and did not lose them anyone.
- A one-time price with ordinary hosting of 15 to 20 EUR a month and no 12-month lock-in, or the START package at around 800 EUR plus hosting, so I can finally take the 2019 programmes off my site without a subscription.
- Sell me the two automations without the website, show me a live demo on my own intake form, and give me one reference from a consultancy that has run the document-chasing flow with real farmers for at least three months.
- Sell me the document-upload link with the automatic reminders on its own, around 500-700 EUR setup and 30-40 a month, without the website; that I'd try, because chasing attachments is where my hours really go.
- A named second person or company who takes over support and the automations if he stops, written into the contract, plus a reference call with one of the first two clients after they have been live for six months.
- Sell me the two automations on their own, plugged into the site I already have, for under 1,000 EUR setup, and show me one Romanian grant consultant who has been using them for six months.
- A grant consultant in Bihor or Bucharest I can phone, who has run the document automation for at least a year with farmer and SME clients and tells me the chasing really dropped.
- Sell me only the two automations, without the site, for a few hundred euros setup and under 60 a month, and let me call one consultant in Bihor who has used the document-upload link with actual farmers for at least three months.
