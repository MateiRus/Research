ILLUSTRUS (Oradea / Bucharest) sells one thing to consultants in European funds: "Dosar Complet" — your clients' documents collected on time, without you chasing them.

How it works: each of your clients gets a personal link, sent on WhatsApp in your firm's name; they upload files or photos from the phone; you see for every client what is still missing; reminders go out automatically on the days you set; a one-page monthly report shows documents collected, reminders sent and days saved. It plugs into your existing website and WhatsApp; no new website is needed. Optional add-on (+400 EUR): new requests from your site or e-mail are checked for missing data and obvious criteria (county, CAEN, firm size) and flagged for you; the eligibility decision stays with you, and the add-on is switched on only after a test on 20–50 of your real past requests that you review.

Price: 900 EUR setup + 50 EUR/month, excluding VAT, month to month, 30 days' notice, no 12-month commitment. The first two clients pay 720 EUR setup, until 31 January 2027, in exchange for a named case study and two introductions. There is no third discounted slot: the founder can deliver one client every six weeks.

Continuity: everything is installed on accounts you own (domain, hosting, automation server, file storage), with a written handover document; the contract names a backup person who takes over support if the founder stops. Support: same working day until 18:00 in the week before any announced submission deadline; otherwise next working day. If an automation fails, the link falls back to a plain upload form and e-mail, so your client is never stuck.

Guarantee: if after 60 days the time between your request and a complete file has not dropped (measured before and after on your real cases), the setup fee is refunded.

What it does not promise: new clients, Google rankings, or eligibility verdicts. The founder is a database developer by day and works on this 10–12 hours a week; the first client is a grant consultant in Bihor who will be available as a phone reference after the 60-day pilot.
